The people behind the idea.

Led by Allie Crawford, marketing consultant and creative director, with a team built around each client.

Why Crawford Connected

Crawford Connected began in social media strategy and management, building brands from the ground up. That work still shapes how we approach every project.

Many people come to us when their work has stopped feeling like theirs. We help them connect to their own business, their purpose or their energy within an idea, sometimes for the first time, sometimes again.

How we work

Allie leads every project. She does the first piece herself: getting to the root of the idea, then building the strategy and the system around it.

Then we bring in the right people for the job, chosen for fit, skills and need. That might be a videographer, an editor or a designer. It’s a custom team, created around your needs and goals. Every job is different.

Questions people ask

Who do you usually work with?

Founders, speakers, educators and organizations with something worth sharing. Often they have the idea, and maybe the end point, but the middle is lost. Some are short on time. Some are missing a skill.

How is this different from an agency or a freelancer?

An agency can feel transactional, with the same approach for everyone. A freelancer is hired for one job. We work as your partner: we look at the idea, the business and the outcome you want, then build a custom team around it.

For creatives

Are you a videographer, editor, designer or another kind of creative? We’d love to meet you. Tell us what you do and where we can see your work, and we’ll reach out when a project fits.
