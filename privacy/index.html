Privacy

Draft, for Anthony and Allie to check

What we collect

When you send the form on our Start a conversation page, we get what you type in. For clients, that’s your name and email, and any of the optional answers you choose to give, such as your business name, your links and what you’d like to move forward. For creatives, it’s your name, your email, what you do, a link to your work and your note.

If you email us, we have what you send that way.

How we use it

Only to reply to you: to set up a discovery call, to prepare for it, and to keep a record of our work together if you hire us. For creatives, to reach out when a project fits your skills.

We don’t sell or rent your details, and we don’t add you to a mailing list.

Who handles it for us

This website is hosted by Cloudflare, which also checks that each form came from a person, not a spam program. The form is delivered to our inbox, info@crawfordconnected.ca, by Resend, an email service. Resend keeps a copy of each email it sends for 30 days. They handle your details only to do that job for us.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits to each page. It uses no cookies and doesn’t tell us who you are.

If a project on our Work page has a video, it comes from YouTube and loads only when you press play.
